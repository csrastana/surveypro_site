Features

Everything you need for field research

SurveyPro supports the full research workflow — from questionnaire design to data collection, storage, quality control, and visualization.

Flexible questionnaire builder

Use multiple question types, skip logic, media in answer options, and custom survey branding. Configure the questionnaire to match your research methodology.

Interview geolocation

Automatically capture interview coordinates. Use an interactive map to monitor fieldwork and verify collected data.

Interview audio recording

Record interviews in the background with participant consent. Audio files are stored securely in the cloud and can be reviewed from the supervisor dashboard.

Real-time analytics

View charts for each question, an interactive location map, and response activity over time. Monitor fieldwork progress in real time.

Photo capture during interviews

Interviewers can capture and upload photos during the survey. Images are stored in the cloud and linked to the corresponding response.

CSV data export

Export all responses in a structured tabular format for further analysis in Excel, SPSS, Stata, or R. Filter and sort data directly in the platform.

How it works

From questionnaire to results in four steps

1

Build your questionnaire

Add questions, configure skip logic, and upload your branding. The visual editor is designed to be intuitive from the start.

2

Publish and share

Generate a survey link for interviewers or respondents. No installation is required — surveys run directly in the browser.

3

Collect field data

Interviewers collect data on tablets or smartphones. GPS, audio, and photos can be captured as part of the interview.

4

Monitor and analyze

Monitor charts, maps, and response tables in real time. Export CSV files for deeper analysis in your preferred statistical software.
